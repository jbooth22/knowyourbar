Frequently asked questions

Are RXBARs healthy?

RXBARs score among the highest for ingredient quality in our database, with most flavors earning an A (Clean) grade. Their whole-food approach using dates, egg whites, and nuts as primary ingredients translates directly to high scores. Natural sugar from dates is the main nutritional consideration.

Do RXBARs have artificial sweeteners?

No. RXBARs contain zero artificial sweeteners, no sugar alcohols, and no artificial flavors. All sweetness comes from dates, making them one of the few mainstream protein bars that rely entirely on whole-food sweeteners.

How much protein is in an RXBAR?

RXBARs contain 12g of protein per bar, sourced from egg whites and nuts rather than protein isolates. This is lower than many protein bars but comes entirely from whole food sources. If maximizing protein per bar is your goal, other options score higher on that metric.

Why do RXBARs have so much sugar?

RXBAR sugar comes entirely from dates, a whole food. A typical RXBAR contains 13 to 18g of sugar, which is higher than bars using artificial sweeteners. The distinction is that this is natural fruit sugar rather than added refined sugar or synthetic sweeteners. Our scoring system reflects the whole-food source positively.

Are RXBARs good for weight loss?

RXBARs are a clean, whole-food option but are calorie-dense at 200 to 260 calories per bar with 12g protein. Bars with higher protein-to-calorie ratios may serve weight loss goals better. That said, RXBAR scores well on ingredient quality and avoids all synthetic additives.
