Are RXBARs Healthy?

Our Verdict
✅ Yes - one of the cleaner options

RXBARs consistently score A (Clean) or B (Good) on ingredient quality. Their whole-food formula of egg whites, dates, and nuts earns genuine positive marks across most flavors.

The tradeoff is macro profile: natural sugar from dates runs higher than most protein bars, and protein content is lower than competing options.

Bottom line: Strong on ingredients. Weaker on protein-to-sugar ratio.

Why reviewing every flavor matters

Most protein bar reviews pick one or two flavors and call the brand done. That misses the point. Within a single brand, ingredient quality can vary significantly by flavor based on protein source choices, sweetener systems, oil selections, and coating ingredients. We scored every RXBAR flavor using the same objective ingredient quality scoring system we apply across our entire 672-bar database.

RXBAR built their brand around radical ingredient transparency - egg whites, dates, nuts, no BS. That philosophy holds up well under scoring. The whole food base earns genuine positive marks from our system. Where some flavors diverge from their clean image is in the coating and flavoring layer, where chocolate coatings and compound ingredients introduce more processing.

Bottom line on RXBAR bars

RXBARs are a legitimately cleaner option than most protein bars. The ingredient quality scores reflect a whole-food first approach that few brands in this category match. The tradeoff is macro profile: natural sugar from dates is still sugar, and protein content is lower than dedicated protein bars. If ingredient quality is your primary filter, RXBAR scores well. If you are optimizing for protein per gram of sugar, other options in our database rank higher.
