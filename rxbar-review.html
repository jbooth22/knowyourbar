Are RXBAR Bars Healthy? We Scored All 12 Flavors

RXBAR scores higher on ingredient quality than almost any brand we track: 10 of 12 flavors earn an A, and the other two land at B, for a full A-to-B range with nothing lower. Every flavor skips artificial sweeteners and sugar alcohols entirely, leaning on dates, honey, or agave nectar instead. The tradeoff shows up in the macros, not the ingredients: protein and fiber land in the middle of the pack, and average sugar runs higher than 85% of bars we've scored. The data below shows exactly where RXBAR stands, flavor by flavor.

RXBAR's standout number is what's absent: zero grams of sugar alcohol and zero artificial sweeteners across every flavor, a combination only RXBAR and Clif Builders manage in our brand comparison below. What the data shows across all 12 RXBAR flavors

RXBAR's lineup is built around whole-food protein and natural sweeteners instead of protein isolates and sugar alcohols. None of the 12 flavors contain sucralose, erythritol, or any other artificial sweetener or sugar alcohol, and that consistency shows up in the grades: 10 flavors land at A, the other two at B, and nothing scores lower.

The lineup really splits into three groups by protein source, even though RXBAR doesn't label them as separate lines. Seven original whole-food bars (Coconut Chocolate, Chocolate Sea Salt, Blueberry, and 4 others) lead with dates and egg whites, carry 12g protein, and score 9.1 to 10.3, the strongest range in the lineup: none of them trip the Sweetener Heavy flag. A newer, higher-protein pair (Strawberry Peanut Butter, Vanilla Peanut Butter) swaps in pea protein and agave nectar to push protein up to 18g, the highest in the lineup, but that agave shows up early enough in the ingredient list to trip our "Sweetener Heavy" check and pull both flavors down to a B. A third group of 3 oats-and-honey flavors (Dark Chocolate Peanut Butter, Blueberry Cashew Butter, Honey Cinnamon Peanut Butter) carries the least protein at 10g and also trips Sweetener Heavy, this time on honey rather than agave, but still scores 9.7 to 10.2, right alongside the original bars. 5 of 12 flavors overall trigger the Sweetener Heavy flag: the 2-flavor pair plus all 3 of the oats-and-honey group, never any of the 7 original whole-food bars.

Bottom line on RXBAR

RXBAR earns its reputation as one of the cleanest lineups we score. Zero artificial sweeteners, zero sugar alcohols, and 10 of 12 flavors land at A. Compare that to Quest or Barebells, where every flavor carries sucralose or a sugar alcohol, and RXBAR's ingredient list advantage is real, not marketing.

The tradeoff is macros, not ingredients. At 12.5g protein and 4.7g fiber per bar on average, RXBAR sits in the middle of our database on both, well behind protein-forward brands like Quest. Sugar averages 14.2g per bar, mostly from dates, honey, or agave, which ranks in the bottom 15% of bars we track for sugar content even though none of it is added table sugar. Which flavor makes sense for you depends more on which of RXBAR's three flavor groups fits your goal than on any single "best" bar. See the picks below.
