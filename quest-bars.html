Are Quest Bars Healthy? We Ranked All 16 Flavors

Quest bars rank in the top 4% for fiber and top 24% for protein across 1,000+ bars we've scored. Every flavor earns a B or C ingredient grade, with scores ranging from 1.1 to 6.2 and no flavor currently reaching an A. The catch: every single flavor also contains artificial sweeteners and sugar alcohols, no exceptions. The data below shows exactly where Quest stands, flavor by flavor.

Quest's standout numbers are fiber and protein efficiency: averaging 12.1g of fiber and 11g of protein per 100 calories puts it in the top 4% and top 6% of all bars we track, respectively. The tradeoff is sugar alcohol: at 6.5g average, Quest carries more sugar alcohol than 88% of bars in the database, which keeps sugar low but can affect digestion for some people.

What the data shows across all 16 Quest flavors

Quest's lineup is one of the most consistent we've scored. All 16 flavors lead with the same milk protein isolate and whey protein isolate blend, all 16 use erythritol as the primary sweetener, and every flavor contains sucralose. Macros barely move flavor to flavor: 18 to 21g of protein, 1 to 2g of sugar, 170 to 200 calories. Every flavor also clears our bar for a genuine protein source, which is why none of the 16 flavors scores below a C.

Where flavors actually differ is additive load, not protein or sweetener choice. Oatmeal Chocolate Chip, Chocolate Peanut Butter, and Double Chocolate Chunk keep a shorter list of add-ons and land at the top of the B range. Birthday Cake, Lemon Cake, and the Dipped varieties bring in palm kernel oil and extra stabilizers, tripping the Processed Oils flag that hits 11 of 16 flavors and pulling their scores into C territory. That split shows up in the grade distribution too: 6 flavors clear B, the other 10 sit at C, and none currently reach an A.

Bottom line on Quest

Quest earns its reputation as a high-protein, low-sugar bar. Averaging 12.1g of fiber per bar is a genuine standout in any category, ranking in the top 4% of bars we track. But the ingredient quality grades tell a more mixed story than the marketing suggests: 6 of 16 flavors land at B, the other 10 at C, and none currently reach an A.

The limitation is just as consistent: artificial sweeteners and sugar alcohols in every flavor, no exceptions. If those ingredients are a dealbreaker, Quest is not your bar regardless of which flavor you pick.
