Are Quest Bars Healthy? We Scored All 16 Flavors

Quest bars rank in the top 3% for fiber and top 23% for protein across 1,000+ bars we've scored. Every flavor earns a B or C ingredient grade, with scores ranging from 1.7 to 6.2 and no flavor currently reaching an A. The catch: every single flavor also contains artificial sweeteners and sugar alcohols, no exceptions. The data below shows exactly where Quest stands, flavor by flavor.

What the data shows across all 16 Quest flavors

Quest's lineup is one of the most consistent we've scored. Every flavor leads with milk protein isolate and whey protein isolate, every flavor uses erythritol as its primary sweetener, and every flavor contains sucralose. The macros are predictable across the board: 18-21g protein, 1-2g sugar, 170-200 calories. So are the ingredient concerns.

Where flavors differ is in additive load. Simpler ones like Oatmeal Chocolate Chip and Double Chocolate Chunk have tighter ingredient lists and land at the top of the B range. More complex flavors like Birthday Cake, Lemon Cake, and the Dipped varieties add palm kernel oil, soluble corn fiber, and extra stabilizers, which pulls their scores down into C territory. The gap between best and worst is 4.5 points (6.2 vs. 1.7), and none of the 16 flavors currently reach an A grade.

Bottom line on Quest

Quest earns its reputation as a high-protein, low-sugar bar. Averaging 12g of fiber per bar is a genuine standout in any category, ranking in the top 3% of bars we track. But the ingredient quality grades tell a more mixed story than the marketing suggests: 9 of 16 flavors land at B, the other 7 at C, and none currently reach an A.

The limitation is just as consistent: artificial sweeteners and sugar alcohols in every flavor, no exceptions. If those ingredients are a dealbreaker, Quest is not your bar regardless of which flavor you pick. If you are fine with sucralose and erythritol and you are prioritizing protein and fiber per calorie, Quest is still one of the more macro-efficient options in our database. Start with Oatmeal Chocolate Chip, Double Chocolate Chunk, or Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough. These three score highest in the lineup and have noticeably shorter ingredient lists than the rest.
