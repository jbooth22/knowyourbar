Quest bars rank in the top 5% for fiber and top 27% for protein across 1,000+ bars we've scored. Every flavor earns a B or A ingredient grade. The catch: every single flavor also contains artificial sweeteners and sugar alcohols, no exceptions. The data below shows exactly where Quest stands, flavor by flavor.

What the data shows across all 16 Quest flavors

Quest's lineup is one of the most consistent we've scored. Every flavor leads with milk protein isolate and whey protein isolate, every flavor uses erythritol as its primary sweetener, and every flavor contains sucralose. The macros are predictable across the board: 18-21g protein, 1-2g sugar, 170-200 calories. So are the ingredient concerns.

Where flavors differ is in additive load. Simpler ones like Oatmeal Chocolate Chip and Chocolate Peanut Butter have tighter ingredient lists and land in A territory. More complex flavors like Birthday Cake, Lemon Cake, and the Dipped varieties add palm kernel oil, soluble corn fiber, and extra stabilizers, which pulls their scores down toward the low B range. The gap between best and worst is nearly 5 points (9.3 vs. 4.4), meaningful even though both are passing grades.

Quest's standout number is fiber. Averaging 12g per bar puts them in the top 5% of all bars we track. Bottom line on Quest

Quest earns its reputation as a high-protein, low-sugar bar. Averaging 12g of fiber per bar is a genuine standout in any category, and every single flavor holds a B or A ingredient grade. No Quest flavor falls into mediocre territory, which is a consistency most brands cannot match across their full lineup.

The limitation is just as consistent: artificial sweeteners and sugar alcohols in every flavor, no exceptions. If those ingredients are a dealbreaker, Quest is not your bar regardless of which flavor you pick. If you are fine with sucralose and erythritol and you are prioritizing protein and fiber per calorie, Quest is one of the stronger options in our database. Start with Oatmeal Chocolate Chip, Chocolate Peanut Butter, or Double Chocolate Chunk. All three earn A grades and have noticeably shorter ingredient lists than the rest of the lineup.
