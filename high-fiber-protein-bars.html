What actually pushes a bar past 11g of fiber

Fiber on a protein bar label comes from one of two places: whole-food ingredients (nuts, seeds, legumes, whole grains) or an isolated fiber additive blended in specifically to raise the number. Among the 103 bars that clear our 11g Extreme Fiber cutoff, 90.3% contain at least one of the five named added-fiber ingredients we checked for.

Tapioca Fiber is the single most common source, showing up in 37 of the 103 Extreme Fiber bars (35.9%). Soluble Corn Fiber, Polydextrose, Chicory Root / Inulin, and Isomalto-oligosaccharide (IMO) follow, often two or three stacked together in the same bar. None of these are red flags on their own, they're standard, well-tolerated ways to add fiber, but they're worth knowing about if a bar's ingredient list otherwise reads short and simple except for one long fiber name.

The three tiers of high fiber

11g of fiber is a real jump, not every bar needs to clear it. The FDA's own labeling rule sets 5g as the cutoff for an "excellent source of fiber" claim, and most people looking for a higher-fiber bar are better served starting there rather than at the Extreme tier above. We track three cutoffs on the same field (Dietary Fiber (g) per bar), cumulative, not separate lists: every Extreme Fiber bar also counts as Very High Fiber and High Fiber.

Where the cutoffs land, recomputed fresh against the live database:
  • High Fiber, 5g+: 612 bars (46.8% of the database), 128 brands. The FDA's "excellent source of fiber" line. A gentle, easy starting point if you're new to eating more fiber.
  • Very High Fiber, 8g+: 243 bars (18.6% of the database), 62 brands. Roughly a third of a typical daily fiber target in one bar.
  • Extreme Fiber, 11g+: 103 bars (7.9% of the database), 24 brands. The tier ranked in full below. Most bars at this level lean on at least one added fiber ingredient to get there (see the category breakdown above).

A good High Fiber example that isn't also Very High or Extreme: Healthy Eating on the Go Chia Seed, 5g fiber, grade A.

A good Very High Fiber example that doesn't reach Extreme: PEAK Protein Dark Chocolate Mint, 9g fiber, grade A.
