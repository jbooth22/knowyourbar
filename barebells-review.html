Are Barebells Bars Healthy? We Scored All 25 Flavors.

Barebells do not contain erythritol, but every one of their 25 flavors contains maltitol, a sugar alcohol with a different but related effect profile. That distinction matters for anyone researching sugar alcohol content specifically. On the macro side, Barebells are hard to argue with: 20g protein averaging just 200 calories, with only 1–2g of sugar per bar. The ingredient quality grades tell a more complicated story: grades range from A down to F across the lineup, largely driven by how different flavors handle their sweetener blend and protein source. Here's the full breakdown of every flavor.

Short answer: Barebells score in the B (Good) range, which is respectable. Their protein content is high (20g), their calorie count is reasonable, and several flavors earn A grades. The concern is consistent: artificial sweeteners and sugar alcohols appear across the lineup, and the coating-heavy flavors carry more processed ingredients.

Bottom line on Barebells

Barebells occupy a specific niche well: high protein, low sugar, candy bar taste. They earn B grades consistently, with several flavors reaching A territory. If you want maximum protein with a dessert-like experience and are comfortable with artificial sweeteners and sugar alcohols, Barebells are one of the better choices in the category.

Frequently asked questions

Are Barebells bars healthy?

Barebells score B (Good) overall with several A (Clean) flavors. They deliver 20g of protein at around 200 calories with minimal sugar (0 to 2g). Every flavor contains artificial sweeteners and sugar alcohols, which is the main consideration for health-conscious consumers.

Do Barebells have artificial sweeteners?

Yes. Every Barebells flavor contains acesulfame potassium and most contain sucralose or other sweeteners. These keep the sugar content extremely low but are a concern for consumers avoiding synthetic sweeteners.

How much protein is in Barebells?

Barebells contain 20g of protein per bar, sourced primarily from milk protein. This puts them in the top tier for protein density across all 900+ bars we track, which is their strongest macro attribute.

Are Barebells good for weight loss?

Barebells score well for weight loss macros: 20g protein at 200 calories with minimal sugar puts them among the most protein-efficient bars we track. The tradeoff is artificial sweeteners and sugar alcohols in every flavor.

Why do Barebells taste like candy bars?

Barebells use coatings, fillings, and caramel layers similar to candy bar construction, with the sugar replaced by erythritol and acesulfame. The result is a bar that closely mimics candy bar texture and sweetness while staying very low in actual sugar.
