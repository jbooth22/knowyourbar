Four caffeine zones, measured against a cup of coffee

Every bar on this page states its caffeine content on the label. We grouped all 42 into four zones based on how that dose compares to a standard 8oz cup of brewed coffee, which runs roughly 95mg. Most bars sit in light-to-moderate territory, but a small handful reach well past a typical energy drink threshold.

11 bars26.2% of caffeinated bars

Frequently asked questions

An average brewed 8oz cup of coffee runs roughly 95mg of caffeine. Bars on this page range from 20mg, a fraction of a cup, up to 350mg, more than three cups or close to a strong energy drink. Most fall in the light-to-moderate range, but a handful of outliers run much higher, so check the zone before you buy.
80mg sits close to a standard cup of drip coffee. For comparison, a shot of espresso is roughly 60-65mg and many energy drinks run 150-300mg. Most bars in our database that carry caffeine land in this same light-to-moderate range rather than energy-drink territory, though a small handful reach 175-350mg.
Some bars source caffeine from actual coffee, matcha, or green tea, which arrives along with other plant compounds. Others list plain caffeine, sometimes as caffeine anhydrous, added directly to the formula. Both raise the same total caffeine number on the label, but the ingredient list tells you which route a bar took, and that shows up in our ingredient scoring.
That depends on your total daily caffeine intake from everything else you consume, and on your personal sensitivity. The FDA generally cites 400mg per day as a reasonable upper limit for healthy adults. A light or moderate bar is a small fraction of that, but the 3 bars on this page running 175-350mg can approach or exceed that limit on their own, especially stacked on top of your usual coffee or tea.
Caffeine is a narrow, deliberate formulation choice, not a byproduct of other ingredients, so a brand has to build a bar around it on purpose. Only 42 of 1,245 bars in our database declare any caffeine at all. Most major brands like Quest, Barebells, and Clif's core lineup skip it entirely and let people pair a regular bar with their own coffee instead.
