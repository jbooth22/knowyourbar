How We Score

Who's behind Know Your Bar

I'm Jeff Booth. I eat protein bars, and I got tired of not knowing what was actually in them. Know Your Bar is what came out of that research.

Who I am, and who I'm not

I'm a regular consumer. I buy these bars, eat them, and started reading the labels closely enough that I wanted a better way to compare them. That turned into a database of 1,000+ bars, each graded A to F on ingredient quality.

I'm not a dietitian, doctor, or any kind of medical professional. Nothing on this site is medical advice. If you're choosing food around a health condition, like diabetes or a GLP-1 medication, talk to your doctor or a registered dietitian. Our guides show you what's on the label. They can't tell you what's right for your body.

How the data is built

Every bar in the database has its full nutrition facts and ingredient list. I use AI-assisted tools to collect and check that data, and I review what they flag. When we find a label has changed, we update the bar.

The A to F grade comes from a rule-based score, not from anyone's opinion. Each ingredient is matched to a scoring table, weighted by where it sits on the label, and the total becomes one letter grade. The same algorithm runs on every bar. Nobody hand-adjusts a grade.

The score has limits. It rates ingredient quality, not taste, macros, or how a bar fits your diet. It isn't precise enough to split two bars in the same grade, so when we pick the best bars for a guide we treat everything in the same grade as equal and break ties with real numbers like protein and sugar.

The full method is on How we score.

Independence

  • No paid placements. No brand can pay to be listed, ranked, or picked.
  • No sponsored picks. Every "best" pick on a guide comes from a rule that's written on that page.
  • One algorithm for every bar. A brand we earn a commission from gets graded exactly like one we don't.

How the site makes money

Some buy buttons are affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a small commission from Amazon or from the brand, at no extra cost to you. That's how the site pays for itself.

Commissions never affect a grade or a rank. Brands that pay a higher commission don't score higher, and bars with no affiliate link at all show up in our lists whenever they win on the rules.
