What disqualifies a protein bar from being dairy free

We check the Dairy Free (Y/N) label on file for every bar, then cross-check ingredient lists ourselves for whey, milk, and casein, the three named dairy sources that show up most in the data. 247 of 1,308 bars, about 19%, carry a dairy free label. The other 1061, about 81%, don't.

Whey is the most common named source at 530 bars (40.5% of the full database), usually as the bar's main protein. Milk shows up in 434 bars (33.2%), and casein in 198 bars (15.1%). The remaining 466 bars, 35.6% of the full database, show no whey, milk, or casein in their own ingredient list at all. They just aren't labeled dairy free, which is a different claim from actually containing dairy.
