What we mean by net carbs

Net carbs is the number that matters for keto: the carbohydrates that actually hit your blood sugar. Fiber passes through without being absorbed. Sugar alcohols have partial or zero glycemic impact. So the formula is:

Total Carbohydrates − Dietary Fiber − Sugar Alcohols = Net Carbs

We applied this to nutrition label data across 900+ bars. We drew the line at 5g net carbs, which fits comfortably in most keto budgets of 20 to 50g daily.

The maltitol caveat: Not all sugar alcohols are equal on keto. Erythritol (used by Quest) has near-zero glycemic impact and most people subtract it fully. Maltitol (used by Barebells and FITCRUNCH) has roughly half the glycemic impact of sugar. Strict keto followers often only subtract half of it, which would push some of those bars over 5g. We follow the standard nutrition label math. Just something to keep in mind if you find certain bars stalling you.

122 of 904 scored bars hit 5g net carbs or fewer. Of those, 86 achieve it through sugar alcohols or artificial sweeteners. Only 36 get there without any synthetic sweeteners. The keto bar market is smaller than most people think.
Quest has the deepest keto lineup. All 16 Quest flavors qualify, averaging 3.8g net carbs at 20g protein. The downside is they all use sucralose and erythritol. If you just track net carbs, Quest is the easiest brand to shop.
David wins on protein per net carb. David bars hit 1 to 4g net carbs at 20 to 28g protein in a 150 calorie bar. Impressive numbers, but they use acesulfame potassium and maltitol to get there. Worth knowing before you commit to a case.
IQ Bar is the standout for clean keto. 10 flavors at 2 to 3g net carbs, 12g protein, no artificial sweeteners, no sugar alcohols. They use chicory root and pea fiber to keep net carbs low naturally. Our favorite answer when someone wants keto without the sweetener baggage.
Epic bars hit 0 to 2g net carbs, but they're meat bars. If you're open to savory, Epic is the lowest net carb option in the database with no sweeteners at all. Not for everyone, but genuinely keto-compatible and real-food based.
Maltitol bars deserve more scrutiny than they get. FITCRUNCH, NuGo, and Barebells all use maltitol. The net carb numbers look good on paper, but anecdotally these bars cause blood sugar responses that erythritol-based bars don't. We flagged every one on this page.
The Feel Bar is easy to overlook. 4 flavors at 4g net carbs, 10g protein, 100 calories, no artificial sweeteners, no sugar alcohols, A grade. Very small brand, hard to find in stores, but they scored better than almost everything else in this category.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as net carbs in a protein bar?
Net carbs = Total Carbohydrates minus Dietary Fiber minus Sugar Alcohols. Fiber and sugar alcohols are subtracted because they have minimal impact on blood sugar. For keto, most people target 20 to 50g net carbs per day total, making bars with 5g or fewer a reasonable fit. We calculated this from nutrition label data across 900+ bars.
Are Quest bars keto friendly?
Yes by net carb count. Quest bars average 3.8g net carbs per bar and all 16 flavors hit 5g or fewer. Every Quest flavor contains sucralose and erythritol though. If you track only net carbs, Quest works well on keto. If you prefer to avoid artificial sweeteners entirely, IQ Bar and Mosh are cleaner alternatives in a similar net carb range.
What keto protein bars have no artificial sweeteners?
Our favorites in this category: IQ Bar at 2 to 3g net carbs with 12g protein, Epic meat bars at 0 to 2g net carbs, Nick's at 3g net carbs with 15g protein, Mosh at 4 to 5g net carbs, The Feel Bar at 4g net carbs with 10g protein, and B.T.R. Nation at 4 to 5g net carbs. Of the 122 bars with 5g or fewer net carbs, 36 have no artificial sweeteners.
Do sugar alcohols count on keto?
It depends which one. Erythritol (Quest) has near-zero glycemic impact and most keto practitioners subtract it fully. Maltitol (Barebells, FITCRUNCH, NuGo) has roughly half the glycemic impact of sugar, so many strict keto people only subtract half of it, which would push some bars over 5g. We follow the standard label math throughout this page. If you're getting kicked out of ketosis by a bar that looks clean on paper, check whether it's using maltitol.
What is the lowest net carb protein bar?
Epic Venison Sea Salt Pepper and Epic Beef Sea Salt and Pepper both have 0g net carbs with no sweeteners of any kind. They're meat bars, not sweet protein bars. Among more traditional protein bars, David hits 1 to 2g net carbs at 20g protein, and IQ Bar hits 2g net carbs at 12g protein with no artificial sweeteners.
