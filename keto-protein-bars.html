How we calculate it

What counts as a keto protein bar

Net carbs is the standard keto metric — the carbohydrates that actually affect blood sugar. Fiber passes through without being absorbed. Sugar alcohols have partial or zero glycemic impact. So the formula is:

Total Carbohydrates − Dietary Fiber − Sugar Alcohols = Net Carbs

We applied this to nutrition label data across 900+ bars. Null sugar alcohol values are treated as 0. Our threshold for this page is 5g net carbs or fewer — a reasonable fit for most keto budgets of 20–50g daily.

The sugar alcohol caveat: Not all sugar alcohols are equal for keto. Erythritol (used by Quest) has near-zero glycemic impact and is fully subtractable. Maltitol (used by Barebells, FITCRUNCH, NuGo) has roughly half the glycemic impact of sugar — many strict keto practitioners only subtract half of maltitol, which would push those bars above 5g. We calculate by the label; your mileage may vary. Each bar card flags which sweetener type it uses.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What is net carbs in a protein bar?
Net carbs = Total Carbohydrates minus Dietary Fiber minus Sugar Alcohols. Fiber and sugar alcohols are subtracted because they have minimal impact on blood sugar. For keto, most people target 20–50g net carbs per day total, making bars with 5g or fewer a reasonable fit. We calculated net carbs from nutrition label data across 900+ bars.
Are Quest bars keto friendly?
Yes by net carb count — Quest bars average 3.8g net carbs per bar and all 16 flavors in our database hit 5g or fewer. However, every Quest flavor contains sucralose and erythritol. If you follow strict keto and are sensitive to artificial sweeteners or sugar alcohols, that matters. If you just track net carbs, Quest is one of the most keto-efficient bars available.
What keto protein bars have no artificial sweeteners?
Keto-friendly bars with no artificial sweeteners include IQ Bar (2–3g net carbs, 12g protein), Epic meat bars (0–2g net carbs), Nick's (3g net carbs, 15g protein), Mosh (4–5g net carbs), The Feel Bar (4g net carbs, 10g protein), and B.T.R. Nation (4–5g net carbs). Of the 122 bars with 5g or fewer net carbs in our database, 36 contain no artificial sweeteners.
Do sugar alcohols count on keto?
It depends on the type. Erythritol (used by Quest) has near-zero glycemic impact and is generally considered fully subtractable. Maltitol (used by Barebells, FITCRUNCH, NuGo) has roughly half the glycemic impact of sugar — many strict keto practitioners only subtract half of maltitol. Our calculation subtracts all listed sugar alcohols per standard convention, but bars using maltitol may affect blood sugar more than the net carb number suggests.
What is the lowest net carb protein bar?
The lowest net carb protein bars in our database are Epic Venison Sea Salt Pepper (0g net carbs, 12g protein) and Epic Beef Sea Salt and Pepper (0g net carbs, 10g protein). Among higher-protein options, David bars hit 1–2g net carbs at 20g protein, and the full David 28g protein lineup hits 4–5g net carbs at 150 calories.
