What "Keto Guide" actually means

A keto-friendly protein bar needs to hit three thresholds: low net carbs, meaningful protein, and enough fat to fit a keto macro profile. For this guide that means 8g or less net carbs, 10g or more protein, and 8g or more fat. Net carbs are calculated as total carbohydrates minus fiber minus sugar alcohols. 469 bars clear all three. Then we rank them by ingredient quality - because a bar can qualify on macros while still being built on erythritol, sucralose, and palm oil.

Net carbs
≤ 8g
Net carbs = total carbs minus fiber minus sugar alcohols. The average across qualifying bars is negative, reflecting high fiber and sugar alcohol content in many formulas.
Protein
≥ 10g
Lower threshold than our other protein guides because keto prioritizes fat over protein. Most qualifying bars land between 10-20g.
Fat
≥ 8g
Fat is the primary macro in a keto diet. Bars need enough fat to actually fit a keto macro split rather than just having low carbs.

Ranked

Only 83 of 469 qualifying bars earn an A on ingredient quality. The keto macro profile almost requires either sugar alcohols, artificial sweeteners, or both to keep net carbs low while maintaining palatability. Brands that avoid them are worth knowing.
Negative net carbs are common - and misleading. Many bars show negative net carbs because sugar alcohols are subtracted from total carbs. Erythritol is the most common. A -9g net carb figure does not mean the bar has no sweeteners - it usually means it has a lot of erythritol.
Barebells qualifies on macros but not ingredients. 19 Barebells flavors hit the keto thresholds. All use erythritol and sucralose. They score B or below on ingredient quality.
Aloha is the largest A-grade keto brand. 18 flavors qualify with A or B grades. Organic ingredients, pea protein, and coconut sugar. One of the few keto-friendly brands built on whole-food principles.
PEAK Protein is the best high-protein keto option. 20g protein, 18g fat, A-grade ingredients, and no artificial sweeteners. Rare combination in this category.
GoMacro qualifies but is not purpose-built for keto. 14 flavors hit the thresholds. They market as organic and plant-based, not keto. The macro fit is incidental, which is actually a good sign for ingredient quality.
86 brands represented despite strict thresholds. The keto qualifying pool is surprisingly broad. The macro combination filters out many bars but leaves a meaningful range of brands, protein levels, and price points.
