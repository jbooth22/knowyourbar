What we screened for on this page

People on GLP-1 medications managing a smaller appetite usually care about three things above all else:

  • Protein — enough to make a small amount of food count
  • Calories — low enough to fit a reduced appetite without crowding out real meals
  • Sugar — kept low since blood sugar swings can compound GI discomfort

We added three more filters on top:

  • Fiber — enough to support satiety and slow digestion
  • No sugar alcohols — since GI tolerance is a common concern on GLP-1 medications
  • Ingredient grade — an A or B ingredient quality grade

28 of 1,181 bars, 2.4% of the database, clear all six.

We are not doctors, but here is what we filtered on: at least 15g of protein, 200 calories or less, 4g of sugar or less, at least 3g of fiber, zero sugar alcohols, and an A or B ingredient quality grade. Appetite suppression from GLP-1 medications means less food volume overall, so every bite needs to carry more protein relative to its size. 28 of 1,181 bars clear all six.
Reduced appetite means a smaller daily calorie budget, so a snack that eats up 300 to 400 calories can crowd out an entire meal's worth of nutrition. We capped this list at 200 calories per bar so the protein-to-calorie ratio stays favorable even when you can only manage a small amount of food. Qualifying bars average 166.1 calories.
GI tolerance is a common concern on GLP-1 medications, and sugar alcohols are a frequent culprit behind bloating, gas, and digestive discomfort. Rather than trying to weigh which sugar alcohols are better or worse tolerated, we excluded any bar containing them at all. This is a stricter rule than our keto and diabetics guides, which allow low-glycemic sugar alcohols like erythritol; here we treat the digestive-tolerance question, not just the blood-sugar question, as the priority.
It is protein divided by calories, scaled to a 100-calorie basis, so you can compare bars of different sizes on equal footing. A bar with 20g of protein at 250 calories is less efficient than one with 15g at 150 calories, even though the first has more total protein. Qualifying bars average 9.5g of protein per 100 calories.
Fiber slows digestion and supports satiety, which reinforces the effect GLP-1 medications already have on appetite and helps you feel satisfied on a smaller amount of food. We required at least 3g of fiber for every bar on this list, and qualifying bars average 8.1g.
Our A through F grade rates how clean and minimally processed a bar's ingredients are. It is a separate measurement from GLP-1 suitability. A bar can score an A on ingredient quality and still not fit the macro profile people on GLP-1 medications look for, or vice versa.
