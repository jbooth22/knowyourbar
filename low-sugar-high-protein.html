What "Low Sugar · High Protein" actually means

Low sugar and high protein are a popular combination, but they are also in natural tension. Protein adds bulk and cost. Keeping sugar below 5g usually requires either skipping sweeteners entirely or relying on sugar alcohols and artificial sweeteners to make up the difference. That is why this guide ranks bars by ingredient quality after filtering by macros - because a bar can hit the numbers while still being built on sucralose, maltitol, and canola oil. The 24 A-grade bars on this list manage both.

Sugar threshold
≤ 5g
Every bar on this list has 5g or less of sugar. The average is 2.0g - well below most protein bars on the market.
Protein threshold
≥ 15g
Every bar has at least 15g of protein. The average across the list is 19g, with several bars hitting 20-25g.

Ranked

Only 24 bars earn an A grade. The macro filter and the ingredient quality filter together are strict. Gryp, Linear, B.T.R. Nation, Munk Pack, and Legion are the brands making it work without leaning on cheap additives.

Calorie range is wide. Qualifying bars range from 170 to 400+ calories. Protein density varies significantly. Gryp hits 25g protein at 170-195 calories. Off the Farm hits 23g at 450-460. Both qualify, but they serve very different use cases.
42 brands represented. Smaller than other guide pools, but still meaningfully diverse. The macro threshold naturally narrows the field to bars purpose-built for protein.

Best brands for Low Sugar · High Protein

This category is dominated by brands that use artificial sweeteners to hit low-sugar targets. These are the few worth knowing - and the ones to watch out for.

Brands to target
  • Gryp 25g protein, 1-5g sugar, A-grade ingredients across multiple flavors. The most impressive macro-to-ingredient ratio in the category.
  • Linear A-grade, 20g protein, 3g sugar. Straightforward ingredient list with no artificial sweeteners. Limited distribution but worth finding.
  • B.T.R. Nation Multiple A-grade flavors at 15g protein and 2-3g sugar. Uses natural ingredients without relying on erythritol or sucralose.
  • Munk Pack (Sleeper Pick) A-grade, 15g protein, 1g sugar on their Cookie Dough bar. Widely underrated in this category and available on Amazon.
Brands to avoid
  • Barebells 25 flavors qualify on macros but all use erythritol and sucralose. Strong numbers, compromised ingredients.
  • FITCRUNCH 15 flavors qualify on macros. Sucralose, maltitol, and palm oil across the line. D-grade ingredient quality.
  • Wonderslim Qualifies on paper but scores F on ingredients. Heavily processed with multiple artificial additives.
