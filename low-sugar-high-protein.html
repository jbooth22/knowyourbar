Looking for Low Sugar, High Protein Bars?

We folded this guide into two more useful ones. Low sugar and high protein is really two different goals depending on why you want it, so we split the list into Keto Protein Bars and Best Bars for Diabetics, each with its own criteria beyond just the macros.

Best Bars for Diabetics
Filtered for net carbs and sugar impact on blood glucose, not just a low sugar number on the label. Ranked by ingredient quality.

A flat "low sugar, high protein" filter sounds simple, but it misses what most people actually need. If you're eating keto, net carbs and fat matter as much as sugar. If you're managing blood sugar, net carbs and how sugar alcohols behave in the body matter more than the label's sugar line. Both guides start from the same low sugar, high protein base this page used, then add the filters that make the list actually useful for that goal.
