Are Clif Bars Healthy? We Scored All 14 Flavors.

Clif Bars are made with genuinely good ingredients organic rolled oats, whole grains, and real food bases that most protein bars don't use. The problem is the sugar. The original CLIF Bar line averages 257 calories with only 10g of protein, and most flavors carry 20–24g of sugar per bar, which drags ingredient scores into the C and D range despite the whole-food base. Clif Builders are a different story: the protein-focused line averages 20g of protein, and two flavors earn an A grade. If you're buying Clif, which line you choose matters a great deal here's how every flavor scores.

Short answer: Clif Bars are designed as endurance fuel, not everyday protein bars. Their ingredient profiles reflect that: organic whole grains and oats score well, but cane syrup and high sugar content drag scores into C territory for most flavors. They are not a good daily snack bar, but they are a reasonable choice for sustained energy during prolonged activity.

Why flavor-by-flavor scoring matters

A brand review that rates all CLIF Bar bars as one product misses the point. Individual flavors can differ by several points on our ingredient quality scale. We scored every flavor using the same objective system we apply across our entire 900+-bar database.

Most Clif Bar flavors land in C (Okay) territory. The whole grain oats and organic ingredients score positively, but brown rice syrup, cane syrup, and high sugar counts pull scores down. This is by design for an energy bar, not an oversight.

Bottom line on CLIF Bar

Clif Bars are well-made energy bars being evaluated as protein bars, which is not entirely fair. For their intended purpose (endurance fuel during exercise), they are a reasonable choice. For daily snacking or high-protein goals, most other bars in our database offer better macro profiles and ingredient grades.

Frequently asked questions

Are Clif Bars healthy?

Clif Bars score in the C (Okay) range for ingredient quality. They have genuine positives including organic whole grains and oats, but high sugar content and processed sweeteners like brown rice syrup pull scores down. They are better suited as energy fuel during endurance activity than as everyday snacks.

How much sugar is in a Clif Bar?

Clif Bars typically contain 17 to 25g of sugar per bar, averaging around 20g. This comes primarily from brown rice syrup and cane syrup, making them one of the higher-sugar bars in our 900+ bar database. The sugar is designed to provide sustained energy during exercise.

Are Clif Bars good for the gym?

Clif Bars are designed as endurance energy bars, not protein bars. With 9 to 11g of protein and 240 to 270 calories, they are better suited to fueling a long run or bike ride than post-workout recovery. For gym use, bars with higher protein-to-calorie ratios would serve better.

Do Clif Bars have artificial ingredients?

Clif Bars do not contain artificial sweeteners like sucralose or acesulfame. However, they do use processed sweeteners like brown rice syrup and cane sugar, and some flavors contain palm kernel oil and natural flavors. They use organic ingredients throughout, which contributes positively to scores.

What is the best Clif Bar flavor?

White Chocolate Macadamia Nut and Oatmeal Raisin Walnut score highest in our ingredient quality system due to whole grain oats leading the ingredient list and lower additive complexity. The lowest-scoring flavors tend to have more coating and filling complexity.
