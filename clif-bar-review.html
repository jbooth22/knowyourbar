Are Clif Bars Healthy? We Ranked All 28 Flavors Across 3 Product Lines

Clif isn't one bar, it's three separate product lines wearing the same name. Clif Builders is the actual protein bar and ranges from grade B to F depending on flavor. CLIF Bar is a lower-protein energy bar that never breaks C, and Clif Kid ZBar is a children's snack bar that never breaks D.

Rather than labeling macros good or bad, we rank Clif against every bar in our database so you can see where they stand in the full market. These figures are averaged across all 28 flavors from all three Clif lines; see the line-by-line breakdown below for how CLIF Bar, Clif Builders, and Clif ZBar differ from each other.

Averaged across all three lines, Clif runs 12.7g of protein and 15.1g of sugar per bar, numbers pulled down by CLIF Bar and Clif ZBar. Judged on Clif Builders alone, the picture is very different: 19.2g protein average, with the family's only B-grade flavors.

What the data shows across all 28 Clif flavors

Clif sells three distinctly different bars under one brand name, and they don't perform anywhere near the same. Clif Builders averages 19.2g of protein per bar, 9g more per serving than CLIF Bar's average of 10.2g and nearly 4 times Clif ZBar's 5.0g. Only Builders is really built as a protein bar. CLIF Bar is closer to a classic energy bar, and Clif ZBar is marketed as Clif Kid ZBar, a children's snack, not an adult protein product.

Ingredient quality tracks the same split. Clif Builders holds the only B grades in the entire family, Peanut Butter Chocolate and Crispy Almond Salted Caramel, and both lead with soy protein isolate and whey protein concentrate without the heavy sugar stack. The rest of the Builders line falls off a cliff: its five F-grade flavors put three separate sugar sources, cane syrup, cane sugar, and brown rice syrup, right behind the protein, plus palm kernel oil. CLIF Bar never reaches that low, but it never reaches higher than a C either, topping out with Peanut Butter Banana at a modest 1.9 score. None of the 28 flavors across any line use artificial sweeteners or sugar alcohols.

Bottom line

Clif isn't one product to judge, it's three. Clif Builders is the only line worth buying for a genuine protein bar, and even then only two of its 10 flavors grade B: those lead with soy and whey protein without the heavy sugar stack, while its five F-grade flavors pile three sugar sources in right behind the protein. CLIF Bar is a middling energy bar, never bad enough to avoid outright but never good enough to recommend over a dedicated protein bar. Clif ZBar isn't really part of this conversation at all, it's a kids' snack bar sold under the Clif name, and by ingredient quality it's the weakest of the three lines.

If you're shopping the Clif lineup specifically for a protein bar, go straight to Clif Builders and check the flavor before you buy, since its flavors run all the way from B to F. Don't assume "Clif" on the label means the bar you're holding is anything like the others.
